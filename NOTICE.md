## Retrieved SCP Wiki content

AIM does not bundle an SCP database. Article text is retrieved live from the
independent Crom GraphQL API and remains separate from AIM's software.

SCP Wiki article content is licensed under the Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported licence. AIM displays the article title,
the author attribution returned by Crom, the source article URL, and the
licence URL with every article:

https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/

SCP Wiki source: https://scpwiki.com/

AIM is an unofficial reader and is not endorsed by or affiliated with the SCP
Wiki, its authors, or Crom. AIM retrieves text only; it does not retrieve or
display article images. This is especially important for the former SCP-173
image, whose separate restrictions are described by the SCP Wiki licensing
guide.
